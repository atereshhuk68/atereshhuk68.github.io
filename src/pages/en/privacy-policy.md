Data is processed in accordance with Art. 6(1)(a), (b), and (f) of the GDPR, i.e.:

- based on the consent of the data subject
- for the performance of a contract or prior to its conclusion
- in the legitimate interest of the administrator (e.g., own marketing)

Your data may be disclosed only to trusted entities supporting our activities, such as:

All entities process data based on data processing agreements and in accordance with the GDPR.
